Tether — Privacy Policy

Senior Schmoopy · Tether (iPhone & CarPlay)
Last updated: 5 November 2026
Short version: We do not collect your data. Tether has no accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything it needs stays on your phone or goes to the servers you set up.

Senior Schmoopy ("we", "us", "our") makes Tether, an iPhone and CarPlay app for playing music, podcasts and audiobooks from servers that you run or use, such as Music Assistant and Audiobookshelf.

What Tether stores on your device

  • Server addresses and settings — for example the address of your Music Assistant server and your playback preferences. Stored locally in the app's preferences.
  • Sign-in tokens for your servers, kept in the iOS Keychain. If you sign in with a username and password, the password is used once to obtain a token and is not saved.
  • Downloaded podcasts and audiobooks you choose to save for offline listening.
  • A diagnostics log of connection and playback events to help troubleshoot problems. It contains no passwords or tokens, and stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

What Tether sends, and to whom

  • Tether connects only to the servers you enter (your Music Assistant and Audiobookshelf servers). That traffic — including your listening position — is between your phone and your own servers. We cannot see it.
  • When you search for a podcast to add, the request goes to your Audiobookshelf server, which looks it up in the public Apple podcast directory. Tether does not contact that directory directly.
  • Album, show and book artwork is loaded from your own servers.

If you use an address that begins with http:// rather than https://, that traffic is not encrypted. Tether warns you when you enter an http:// address that is on the public internet.

Third parties

Tether contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or third-party tracking SDKs. The only network destinations are the servers you configure yourself. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

Permissions

  • Local network — to reach servers on your home network.
  • Background audio — so playback continues when the screen is off or in CarPlay.

Data retention and deletion

All Tether data is stored on your device. To remove it, delete the app — that removes its settings, tokens, downloads, and diagnostics log. Because we do not collect or retain your data on any server of ours, there is no separate deletion request to make; deleting the app deletes everything.

Children

Tether is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app is intended for use with your own personal media servers.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the "Last updated" date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
